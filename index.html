DEMO 01
OCCLUDED LANDMARK

Occluded Landmark Identification

Key identity details are concealed while architecture and environmental clues remain available.

Prompt: Identify the real-world location within 10 minutes using visible clues and explain the evidence.

Rubric: Correctly identifies the ground-truth location and supports it with at least two independent clues.

DEMO 02
CROPPED PRODUCT

Cropped Product Research

Brand/model identifiers are removed while shape, materials and controls remain visible.

Prompt: Determine the most specific product model within 10 minutes and cite evidence distinguishing it from alternatives.

Rubric: Identifies the correct model and provides evidence that distinguishes it from plausible alternatives.

DEMO 03
MASKED MEDIA FRAME

Masked Media-Frame Research

A simulated frame preserves secondary clues while hiding a primary identifying region.

Prompt: Identify the source media using remaining clues and verify the answer against a reliable source.

Rubric: Identifies the correct source media and verifies it with a reliable independent source.

Research & Ground-Truth Workflow

  1. Inspect the source image and list observable clues.
  2. Create a controlled crop, mask, occlusion or degradation.
  3. Form research hypotheses without leaking the target identity.
  4. Run iterative web and reverse-image research.
  5. Compare independent sources and verify the ground truth.
  6. Prepare prompt, one-sentence rubric and solution notes.

Solution Note Template

Ground truth: [identity]

Source: [original source]

License/provenance: [license + attribution]

Visible clues: [objective observations]

Research path: [searches and comparisons]

Verification: [supporting source]

Expected outcome: [answer + evidence]

Copyright & Provenance Discipline

For real assignments, I would record the source URL, creator/rights holder, license or public-domain status, attribution requirements and any modification made to the image. I would not label an image “copyright-free” merely because it appears in search results; the actual licensing/provenance would be documented.

Creative Commons licenses have specific reuse conditions, and CC-licensed works require attribution; the exact license terms should be checked before reuse. Nigerian-origin material also requires careful attention to applicable copyright status and licensing.

Quality Assurance

Visual Integrity

Mask only intended identity-bearing regions and preserve genuine secondary clues.

Research Integrity

Separate observations from hypotheses and verify the ground truth with reliable evidence.

Prompt Integrity

Keep prompts time-bound, clear and neutral without revealing the target.

Rubric Integrity

Use concise, objective, one-sentence grading criteria.

File Discipline

Use predictable filenames, versions and structured Google Drive folders.

Final Review

Check image quality, provenance, prompt, ground truth, evidence and completeness.
